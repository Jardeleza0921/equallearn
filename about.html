About the app

EqualLearn keeps learning, teaching, and class progress in one place.

EqualLearn is a responsive learning platform created by a group of PTCians. It gives students a simple place to study, teachers practical tools for managing learning content, and administrators a clearer view of users and activity.

What it is for

A practical classroom workspace.

The project focuses on making common learning tasks easier to reach from a laptop, desktop, or Android phone.

For students

Open learning content, take quizzes, review results, track progress, and maintain a student profile.

For teachers

Manage learning content, build quizzes, follow class engagement, and review student learning records.

For administrators

Manage students and teachers, organize classes, review activity, and maintain platform settings.

Built by PTCians

Designed around an actual school project, not a generic demo.

EqualLearn is developed as a Pateros Technological College project. Its interface uses a green visual direction inspired by PTC while keeping the workspace clean, readable, and usable across different screen sizes.

Web and Android

The same learning experience is available through the website and Android APK.

Role-based access

Student, teacher, and administrator workspaces keep tools focused on each role.

Real learning activity

Progress and engagement views use stored platform activity rather than decorative sample values.

Learning content

Modules, lessons, resources, quizzes, and results are organized around classroom use.
